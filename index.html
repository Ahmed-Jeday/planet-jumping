About Planet Jumping

A high-fidelity cinematic simulation combining Canvas 3D projective geometry and orbital astrophysics.

Planet Jumping was conceived as a visceral, borderless space exploration portal. It decouples the viewing aperture from the cosmic scene through a screen-locked clipping projection, transforming a simple web surface into a floating celestial viewport.

Technical Architecture

  • Zero Dependencies: Self-contained vanilla TypeScript/JavaScript with custom 2D Canvas matrix perspective math.
  • Fake 3D Focal Model: 850px focal perspective projection interpolating 44 rounded-corner vertices per frame.
  • Spatial Web Audio Engine: Procedural synthesis of cosmic sub-bass drones and frequency sweep warp effects without external media assets.
  • Progressive Web App: Service Worker precaching with offline resilience.
